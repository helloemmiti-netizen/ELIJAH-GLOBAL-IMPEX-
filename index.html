About Our Company

Built on Trust, Delivered with Integrity

Headquartered in Palakol, West Godavari District, Elijah Global Impex Private Limited is a dedicated export-import house that connects Indian producers with international buyers. We work directly with farmers, processors, and aquaculture facilities across Andhra Pradesh to deliver products that meet the strictest global quality standards.

Under the leadership of Director Elijah Vivek Sanamanda, every shipment undergoes rigorous quality inspection, proper documentation, and end-to-end logistics management — so our partners receive exactly what they ordered, on time, every time.
